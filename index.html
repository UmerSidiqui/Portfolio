about

Backend-first, AI-native.

what I actually do, in plain terms

I architect and ship production-grade backend systems for AI applications — REST APIs, authentication, databases, and the cloud deployment underneath. I own systems end to end: designing the architecture, writing the backend, and reasoning about how it behaves under real traffic. That grounding in core system design (API architecture, database selection, caching, load balancing, security) is what the AI layer sits on top of.

On the AI side, I build agentic workflows with LangGraph, RAG pipelines with hybrid structured + semantic retrieval, and voice AI integrations that need to feel instant on a live phone call. I care about the unglamorous parts too — LLMOps, cost and latency trade-offs, and making sure a demo becomes something that survives production traffic.
